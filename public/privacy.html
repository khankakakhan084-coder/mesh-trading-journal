Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Mesh Trading Journal ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a personal trade tracking platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application at https://adil-trading-journal.vercel.app.

2. Information We Collect

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In, we receive basic profile information provided by Google with your permission:

  • Your primary email address
  • Your name and profile picture
  • A unique user identifier (UID) generated by authentication services

We do not access your Google contacts, emails, or Google Drive files.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure access to your trading logs.
  • Associate your logged trades, notes, screenshots, and metrics with your private account.
  • Display your personalized dashboard and avatar.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your trade data is securely stored in Supabase cloud database with encrypted row-level security (RLS). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may export your trade logs at any time as JSON or request deletion of your account and associated records by contacting us.
